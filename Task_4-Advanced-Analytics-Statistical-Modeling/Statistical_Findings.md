# Statistical Findings

## Key Insights

1. Technology and Furniture categories show significant differences in profit (t-test).
2. Region and Category are statistically related (chi-square test).
3. Monthly sales display seasonal patterns.
4. Moving averages smooth fluctuations in sales trends.
5. Customers were segmented into 4 clusters using K-Means.
6. PCA successfully visualized customer groups.
7. Linear Regression achieved reasonable predictive performance.
8. Profit and Quantity were among the most important predictors of Sales.
