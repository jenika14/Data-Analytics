# Visualization Findings

## Key Insights

1. Technology category generated the highest sales.
2. Consumer segment contributed the maximum revenue.
3. West region recorded the highest overall sales.
4. California was the leading state in terms of sales.
5. Sales peaked during the year 2016.
6. A few high-value products contributed significantly to total revenue.
7. Discounts negatively impacted profits in certain categories.
8. Interactive filters helped analyze sales by region, category, and date.
