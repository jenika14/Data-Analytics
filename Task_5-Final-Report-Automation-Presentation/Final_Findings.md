# Final Project Findings

## Executive Summary

The Superstore dataset was analyzed using EDA, SQL, visualization, statistical analysis, clustering, and predictive modeling.

## Top 5 Insights

1. Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
2. The West region contributed the most revenue.
3. California was the top-performing state.
4. Customer segmentation identified 4 distinct customer groups.
5. Monthly sales showed seasonal patterns over time.

## Business Recommendations

1. Increase investment in Technology products.
2. Focus marketing campaigns on high-value customer segments.
3. Optimize discounts to improve profitability.
