# SQL Findings

## Dataset Overview
- Dataset: Sample Superstore
- Database: SQLite
- Total Records: 9994

## Key Insights

1. Technology category generated the highest sales (₹836,154.03).
2. Technology category also produced the highest profit (₹145,454.95).
3. Consumer segment contributed the maximum spending.
4. Binders were the most sold sub-category.
5. California recorded the highest sales among all states.
6. Office Supplies had the highest average discount.
7. The West region generated the highest total sales.
8. Several products had sales significantly above the average sales value.
9. Window functions were used to rank products based on sales.
10. CTEs and subqueries simplified complex business analysis.
