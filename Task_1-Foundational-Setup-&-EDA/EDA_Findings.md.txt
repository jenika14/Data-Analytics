# EDA Findings

## Dataset Overview
- Total Rows: 9994
- Total Columns: 21
- Memory Usage: 1.6 MB

## Data Cleaning
- No missing values were found.
- No duplicate records were present.
- Order Date and Ship Date were converted to datetime format.

## Unique Categories
- Categories: Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology
- Segments: Consumer, Corporate, Home Office
- Regions: South, West, Central, East

## Key Insights
1. Technology category has the highest average sales.
2. Office Supplies has the lowest average sales.
3. West region generates the highest profit.
4. Central region generates the lowest profit.
5. Sales distribution is highly right-skewed, indicating a few very large orders.
6. Discount has a slight negative correlation with profit.
7. The dataset is clean and ready for advanced analysis.
